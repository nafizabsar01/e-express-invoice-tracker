e-Express Invoice Tracker — Privacy

This is an internal tool of ESCM LLC for approved staff only.

Sign-in: "Sign in with Google" is used only to confirm your Google account email address. The app does not request access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.

What is stored: your email address in the list of approved users, and an audit record (your email and time) of any invoice changes you make. Invoice data is stored in a private ESCM LLC Google Sheet and is not shared with third parties.
